## What is Pika UI?

Pika UI is the component library behind the **Gender Reveal Voting App**. It takes
the warmth of an old arcade cabinet — hard 4px borders, a pink-and-gold palette,
`Press Start 2P` display type — and holds every one of those choices to the
**AAA level of WCAG 2.2**: 7:1 text contrast, 3px focus rings, 44px tap targets,
and full reduced-motion support.

## Design principles

<div className="pika-cards">
  <article className="pika-card">
    <h3>Pixel, not plastic</h3>
    <p>Every surface has a hard 4px border and 0px radius. Depth comes from
    stacked box-shadows — the stepped “8-bit” look — never from blur.</p>
  </article>
  <article className="pika-card">
    <h3>Readable retro</h3>
    <p><code>Press Start 2P</code> is reserved for H1 and hero CTAs. UI text runs
    on <code>VT323</code> (CRT terminal) and body copy on <code>Nunito</code>, so
    sentences stay legible.</p>
  </article>
  <article className="pika-card">
    <h3>Accessible by default</h3>
    <p>Components ship with AAA colour pairings, visible focus, large hit areas
    and semantic markup. The a11y addon runs the enhanced contrast check on every
    story.</p>
  </article>
  <article className="pika-card">
    <h3>Token-driven</h3>
    <p>Colour, spacing, type and motion all come from <code>src/tokens</code>,
    mirrored in <code>design-tokens.json</code>. Change a token, not a component.</p>
  </article>
</div>

## Foundations

### Type

| Face | Role | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Press Start 2P** | Display | H1, hero title, primary CTA |
| **VT323** | Subheading | H2–H4, buttons, labels, counters |
| **Nunito** | Body | Paragraphs, inputs, long-form copy |

Decorative tints (`#fc5c94`, `#5c94fc`, …) are for fills and art only. Anything
carrying text uses the `*Strong` token, each verified at ≥7:1.

### Spacing &amp; radius

4 · 8 · 16 · 24 · 32&nbsp;px on a hard grid. All radii are **0px** — pixel art has
no rounded corners.

## Accessibility commitments

<div className="pika-a11y">
  <div><strong>SC 1.4.6 — Contrast (AAA)</strong><span>Body text ≥ 7:1, large text ≥ 4.5:1. Enforced in CI via <code>color-contrast-enhanced</code>.</span></div>
  <div><strong>SC 2.4.7 / 2.4.11 — Focus Visible</strong><span>3px solid ring, 2px offset, never clipped by sticky UI.</span></div>
  <div><strong>SC 2.5.8 — Target Size</strong><span>Interactive controls are at least 44×44px (spec minimum is 24px).</span></div>
  <div><strong>SC 2.2.2 — Pause, Stop, Hide</strong><span><code>prefers-reduced-motion</code> disables pixel-float, pulse, blink and the CRT scanline overlay.</span></div>
  <div><strong>SC 1.3.1 — Info &amp; Relationships</strong><span>Semantic elements, real labels, grouped form controls (<code>FormField</code>, <code>RadioGroup</code>).</span></div>
  <div><strong>SC 1.4.11 — Non-text Contrast</strong><span>Borders, switch tracks and focus rings all clear 3:1.</span></div>
</div>

## Getting around

- **Foundations** — tokens for colour, type, spacing and motion.
- **Atoms** — Button, Card, Input, Badge, Checkbox, Radio, Toggle, Tags, Typography.
- **Molecules** — FormField, RadioGroup, CheckboxGroup, ButtonGroup, CardSection.
- Use the **Accessibility** tab in the addons panel to see the live axe report,
  and the **Backgrounds** toolbar to check components on <em>Manual paper (AAA)</em>,
  pixel pink, or arcade night.
